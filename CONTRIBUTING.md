# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve AI Revenue Recovery OS.

## Before submitting

- Keep all example customer records clearly fictional.
- Keep monetary values in USD unless the product requirements explicitly change.
- Do not claim that demo interactions send messages, process payments, or persist data.
- Do not commit credentials, tokens, personal data, or real invoice details.
- Keep the interface responsive and usable with keyboard navigation.
- Describe what you changed and how you tested it in your pull request.

## Current architecture

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype. Backend, authentication, AI integrations, messaging, and payments are not implemented.
